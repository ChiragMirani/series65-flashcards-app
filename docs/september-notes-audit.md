## Corrections and qualifications

- Rule of 72 estimates doubling time or growth rate; it does not price a coupon bond. The supplied recovery arithmetic is approximately 24.7%, but the copied scenario is not a deck source.
- The existing adviser oral-discretion accommodation is **ten business days after the first transaction**, not generic consent within ten calendar days. Third-party authority is prior written authority.
- NASAA's custody model distinguishes a client's check payable to a third party from inadvertently received client property. Both three-business-day provisions also require records. Ordinary custodian statements are quarterly; the adviser has an inquiry duty. State examination filings go to the Administrator under this model.
- The model Act commercial-paper provision says **nine months**, with the denomination and rating conditions; the insurer provision refers to authorization in **this state**.
- The state model's $5,000/three-year criminal ceiling is not a current-law ceiling for every jurisdiction. Civil recovery is different. Final-order appeal does not itself produce a stay.
- Daily inverse and leveraged objectives do not imply the corresponding return over every holding period. ETFs need not be passive.
- The passive-loss general rule remains paired with suspended-loss release. Gift carryover basis remains paired with the dual-basis exception.
- TIPS fixed coupon does not justify an unconditional claim that every investor's realized real return equals that coupon. Principal indexing and coupon-payment frequency are separate concepts. No new TIPS timeline or vendor numerical example was added.
- A common recommendation can be suitable for multiple clients after individual inquiry. An undifferentiated recommendation process does not satisfy that duty. Lower correlation improves diversification; it is not a guarantee that gains exactly cancel losses.
- Existing annuity and retirement cards use **59½**, not a formatting-damaged 59. A governmental 457(b) distribution's additional-tax treatment is separate from when the plan permits a distribution.

These issues also remain visible in [content conflicts](content-conflicts.md). The [earlier audit](additional-content-audit.md) retains the six unresolved supporting details from the full cram sheet; this later supplement does not claim to resolve them.
